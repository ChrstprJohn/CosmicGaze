# Cosmicgaze showcase

22 seconds, 1920×1080, 30fps. A dedicated premium motion graphics film using fresh captures of Cosmicgaze's homepage, Moon match, cosmic age, Solar System, planet builder, and mobile UI. Browser perspective, directional entrances, simulated clicks, and a final desktop/phone ensemble showcase the actual pages.

`brag.mp4` is the full film with original ambient music and quiet UI sound effects; `cosmicgaze-showcase-muted.mp4` is the website export. `brag.jpg` is the settled ensemble poster, baked into frame zero of both videos. `share-copy.txt` is the posting caption. `composition/` contains the editable Hyperframes composition and local media.

The source is deterministic, uses one paused GSAP timeline, and is checked with Hyperframes 0.8.112. The audio light is driven by pre-extracted frequency bands. Before/after page captures simulate Moon merge and planet selections; date examples are fictional demo inputs.

Website imagery and textures retain their original credits. The homepage galaxy is NGC 1232, FORS / VLT / ESO via NASA APOD. Planet textures: Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0. SFX: Kenney CC0. Fonts: Instrument Serif and DM Sans, from the project's existing local bundles. Music is synthesized locally by make-music.py without samples.

The previous brag-output directory remains a historical archive. Only the new Cosmicgaze film is used by the website.
